# UniCircle — UX / User-Centred Product Design

UniCircle is a user-centred mobile app concept developed to support social connection and belonging among university students, particularly first-year students.

The project included user research, iterative prototyping, and evaluation.

## Overview

In response to the challenge of supporting student social connection and belonging, this project explored how digital product design can help students feel more connected on campus.
I developed UniCircle: an interactive mobile app concept centred on discovering relevant events, coordinating attendance with others, and creating a more personalised student experience.

## Design Focus

The project was grounded in product design, UX, and HCI thinking. The design aimed to create a student experience that felt useful, intuitive, and socially supportive.

Key design considerations included:
- event discovery and visibility
- attendance coordination and social encouragement
- personalisation
- clear navigation and system feedback
- alignment with users’ mental models

## Process

### 1. User Research
I used survey-based user research to better understand the social needs, behaviours, and challenges experienced by students.

### 2. Design & Prototyping
Using Figma, I developed a high-fidelity mobile prototype supported by wireframing, user flows, and iterative design decisions.

### 3. Evaluation
I evaluated the design through task-based think-aloud usability testing, then refined the prototype based on the findings.
